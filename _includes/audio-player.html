{% comment %}Audio player (post.html, under the reader toggle). An entry whose front matter has `audio:` (an MP3 URL), `audio_length:` (bytes) and `audio_duration:` ("43:10") shows a native player here and is listed in /podcast.xml. An entry with a younger-readers text can carry a narration of it too, in `audio_young:`, `audio_young_length:` and `audio_young_duration:`; the player then follows the reader toggle, playing whichever text is showing. Under the player, a row of speed buttons (1× to 2×) and a readout of the time left at that speed; the row stays hidden until static/js/audio-speed.js runs, and the native controls work without it. The files are made with journal-audio/make-audio.py, beside the site repos. Styles: main.css .audio-player.{% endcomment %} {% if page.audio %}
