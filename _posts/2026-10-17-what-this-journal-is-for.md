The Journal of Experiments is a record of other people's experiments and the lessons they took from them.

Each entry recounts one experiment: who ran it, what they were trying to find out, what happened, and what they concluded. The point is the lesson, retold so it outlives the moment.

Delete this entry once the first real recount is in.
